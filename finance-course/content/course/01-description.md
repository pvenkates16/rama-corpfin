title: Course description
---
Corporate finance applies the principles of valuation to decisions made inside firms:
**investment** (capital budgeting), **financing** (capital structure and payout), and
**risk management**.

### Learning outcomes

By the end of the course you will be able to:

- Value projects using discounted cash flow and compare decision rules.
- Estimate the cost of capital for a firm or project.
- Explain how capital structure and payout policy affect firm value.
